# Code of Conduct

BridgeVM welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience
levels. Project spaces should remain useful, respectful, and focused on the
work.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, patient, and constructive.
- Critique claims and code with evidence; do not attack the person presenting
  them.
- Make room for questions and honest corrections.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Accept that a failed experiment or retracted claim is valuable engineering
  work when it is recorded honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly unsafe or deceptive
technical claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project conduct, contact the maintainers through the repository's
moderation tools. For sensitive security or privacy matters, use the private
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
participants when necessary to protect the community and project.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, and other spaces where someone represents BridgeVM.
