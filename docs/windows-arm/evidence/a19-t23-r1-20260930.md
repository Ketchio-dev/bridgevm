ten-lane campaign in the A19 statement, and it is not pooled with the earlier
single samples (T20 r6–r8, T21, T22). The receipt keeps `claim_eligible`,
`criterion_pass`, `capability_promotion` and `three_d_injection` false. It

satisfies the campaign part of A19 is an operator decision. A19 stays OPEN,
